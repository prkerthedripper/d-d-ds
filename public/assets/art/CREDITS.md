# Artwork credits

All images are stored locally under `public/assets/art/` — nothing is hotlinked.
Backgrounds, items and powers were sourced through **Openverse**
(openverse.org), **Wikimedia Commons** and **rawpixel**, filtered to CC0 /
Public Domain Mark. The fantasy character plates are an original generated set;
see `rpg/CREDITS.md` for generation details and licensing notes.

## Character plates — original fantasy RPG illustrations

The class, species and background images under `rpg/` are AI-generated for this
project, not stock photographs. The full set, prompts, seeds and source notes
are documented in:

- `rpg/CREDITS.md`
- `rpg/generation.json`

They are intentionally separate from the modern-Webster photography used by the
world, party and codex screens.
