## Notes

- North Ponds Park is now officially Charles E. Sexton Memorial Park at 750 Holt Road.
  The Town of Webster describes its two ponds, one-mile paved loop, fitness stations,
  disc golf, fishing and lodge. The trail is off Route 104 between Holt Road and Route 250.
- The festival photographs are used as actual park photographs, not as generic scenery.
  Their Commons descriptions identify the Waterfront Art Festival in Webster and their
  coordinates fall inside the park.
- The NY 104 photographs document the real road corridor near the park. They should not
  be captioned as a specific building or business.
- The aerial image is public-domain USDA NAIP imagery displayed through USGS The National
  Map. It is a map reference, not a street-level photograph.
- CC BY-SA images remain available under their original licences. If you redistribute
  modified versions, keep this credit file with them.
