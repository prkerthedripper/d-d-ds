# World terrain assets

The campaign world uses optimized 3D models and PBR textures from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/). Poly Haven releases these assets under the
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) license:
they may be used, modified and redistributed without attribution. Attribution is
kept here anyway so the source of the art is clear.
